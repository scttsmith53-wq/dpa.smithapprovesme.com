Traverse Employee Homebuying Benefit — Important Program Information: The Traverse Employee Homebuying Benefit is available to eligible Traverse employees who purchase a home using Scott Smith as their real estate broker. The real estate benefit is calculated as 0.25% of the final purchase price and is provided as a credit at closing toward eligible buyer closing costs and/or prepaid items where permitted. The benefit is not a cash rebate, has no cash value outside an eligible closing, and no unused portion will be paid directly to the buyer.

The actual credit may be reduced if the buyer has insufficient allowable closing costs or if lender, loan-program, seller-contribution, interested-party-contribution, title, settlement, regulatory or other applicable limits restrict the amount that may be credited. Availability and amount are subject to the purchase contract, loan program, lender approval, settlement requirements, transaction eligibility, final closing documentation and applicable law.

Registration does not obligate an employee to purchase a home, obtain financing, or enter into a real estate representation agreement. Mortgage financing is offered separately through Laura Winkle / Guaranteed Rate and is subject to credit approval and applicable lending requirements. Real estate brokerage services are provided by Scott Smith. Equal Housing Opportunity.
